01 — About DapurKu

Built for the rush,
not the spreadsheet.

A busy service does not wait for admin to catch up. DapurKu gives food-service teams one clear place to see what is ordered, what is in stock, and how the day is tracking.

Instead of scattered notes and end-of-day guesswork, DapurKu turns daily operations into a simple Android workflow. Orders move forward visibly, inventory stays current as service happens, and the numbers are ready when you need them. You can try it all with 30 free tokens before adding credits.

DapurKu was designed and developed by ZYOP Solution, the independent digital studio of founder Azam Ayop — built around the same principle that guides every ZYOP project: useful, considered, and made to last.

02 — Features

The essentials,
in one clear view.

DapurKu keeps the parts of a service day that matter most close together — so the team spends less time piecing information together and more time serving.

Order management

Capture and follow orders in one place, from first entry through to completion — with the current state always easy to see.

Live inventory

See stock move as service happens, so decisions are based on what is actually on hand rather than yesterday's count.

Daily analytics

A straightforward end-of-day view of activity and performance, ready without a separate reporting ritual.

Android-first

Designed for the devices teams already carry on the floor, with a layout that makes sense in the middle of a shift.

Operational clarity

A focused workflow shaped around real food-service operations, not a generic admin template with extra screens to ignore.

Token-based access

Start with 30 tokens free, then simple usage credits at 5 cents per token — no expiry date and no pressure to commit to a large bundle up front.
